About Golden Tours

More than a trip — a lesson in motion.

Golden Tours is a South African educational-tourism company led by Mr Sello David Moloto, with more than 10 years of experience in the educational excursion and tourism environment. We specialise in designing and coordinating excursions for primary schools and high schools — and we also serve companies, churches, families and groups.

Having worked with more than 50 schools, we understand what teachers and school administrators need: reliable planning, safety-conscious coordination, clear communication and experiences that genuinely add value to the curriculum. From the first enquiry to the final drop-off, we handle the detail so educators can focus on the learners.
